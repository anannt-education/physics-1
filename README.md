# Anannt AP Physics 1

Self-study Unit 1 slice for AP Physics 1 (May 2027), mounted at `https://study.anannt.ae/physics-1`.

Two motion / graph-reading lessons are public without an account. Units 2–8 are unpublished. This desk does not predict an official AP score.

## Public without an account

- Subject home: `/physics-1`
- Exam guide: `/physics-1/about`
- Lesson 1 — motion graphs: `/physics-1/lesson/lesson-motion-graphs`
- Lesson 2 — turning point: `/physics-1/lesson/lesson-zero-v-a`
- Diagnostic start: `/physics-1/diagnostic`
- Graph-repair paths may stay public under `/physics-1/repair/...`

After lesson 2, a diagnostic submit, or a visit to practice / FRQ / mocks, the app redirects to `https://study.anannt.ae/start?subject=physics-1`.
